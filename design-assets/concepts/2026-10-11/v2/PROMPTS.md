# Tiga konsep dengan alur berbeda — owner dan kasir

Built-in ImageGen. Angka, akun dan transaksi pada gambar adalah ilustrasi. Ketiga opsi mempertahankan palet yang sama agar perbedaan susunan dan alur terlihat.

## Opsi A

Use case: ui-mockup. Create an original high-fidelity mobile POS and multistore owner app design concept board for "Owner Tenang", in Indonesian. Landscape board, two large complete flat phone screens side by side, owner on left clearly labeled "OWNER", cashier on right clearly labeled "KASIR". Both screens entirely visible including bottom controls, on pale neutral backdrop. Restrained premium product design inspired by Steve Jobs' simplicity, strong hierarchy, minimum decisions, no Apple logo or portrait. Very crisp legible Indonesian typography, believable large touch targets, simple understandable icons. CONSISTENT COLOR PALETTE ACROSS ALL CONCEPTS: near-white, deep navy text, cobalt-blue primary buttons; distinction must come from information architecture, placement and number of buttons, navigation model, and interaction flow, NOT color swaps. No charts, no decorative illustrations, no microscopic labels, no perspective, no glowing gradients. All monetary data and transactions are hypothetical examples. The owner oversees multiple staffed stores and also does admin work. Cashier is already on shift with drawer OPEN; no login, clock-in or drawer-opening action in the sale itself. Product names "Es Teh" at "Rp5.000" and "Leker Cokelat" at "Rp7.000"; example basket two Es Teh plus one Leker Cokelat totals "Rp17.000". Avoid duplicate competing primary actions. CONCEPT A / "Papan tugas". Design a genuinely tile-based task launcher architecture, not a conventional dashboard. Board title "A / Papan tugas", subtitle "Pilih pekerjaan, langsung kerjakan." OWNER screen: small brand, context chip "Semua gerai ⌄", bold heading "Mau mengurus apa?". Dominant 2x2 action tiles occupying middle screen: "Keputusan" with badge "3", "Barang & resep", "Pantau gerai", "Laporan". Each tile has large meaningful icon and label, 44px-plus target, generously separated. Below a narrow single-line daily summary "Penjualan hari ini Rp2.480.000". Lower section a plain compact store list "Mandala · Laci tutup", "Beji · Laci tutup". NO bottom tab bar on owner; only quiet top-right circular account avatar. These four tiles ARE the navigation. KASIR screen: top line "Mandala" and subtle "Laci buka", small history button top right. Large full-width search field "Cari barang". Horizontal category chips "Favorit", "Minuman", "Leker", first selected. Six product tiles in a two-column grid: Es Teh Rp5.000, Leker Cokelat Rp7.000, Kopi Rp8.000, Leker Keju Rp8.000, Teh Hangat Rp4.000, Leker Pisang Rp7.000. Each product tile has a small clear plus button in lower corner, no big photos needed. Lower white fixed basket tray shows two rows with simple minus/count/plus steppers: "Es Teh" quantity2 and "Leker Cokelat" quantity1. ONE full-width blue sticky button "Bayar Rp17.000". No bottom nav beneath payment. Owner is launcher-first; cashier is product-grid-first with basket always visible. Show these structural differences unambiguously.
