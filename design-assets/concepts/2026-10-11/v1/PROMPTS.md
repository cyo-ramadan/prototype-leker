Use case: ui-mockup. Create one exceptionally polished high-fidelity mobile product design concept board for Owner Tenang, an Indonesian small-business app for a business owner who manages multiple stores and also does the admin work, with cashiers employed in each store. Guided by Steve Jobs-era product principles: ruthless clarity, one obvious next action, progressive disclosure, beautiful calm typography, generous touch targets, restrained visual hierarchy. This is an original product concept, not an Apple branded product. All displayed numbers are illustrative. Output landscape image with two large flat mobile screens side by side, fully visible from top status bar to bottom edge. Thin restrained device borders, no perspective, no physical props, no person, no Apple logo, no 3D ornament, no watermark. Crisp legible Indonesian text. Each phone has realistic 390x844 proportions. Show only relevant features, no technical accounting jargon, no admin/owner role switching buttons. The right phone always demonstrates entering a recipe directly within an item, never navigating to a separate recipe screen. No detached recipe-link workflow.
